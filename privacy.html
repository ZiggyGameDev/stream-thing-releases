Privacy Policy

DizziTV is built to know as little about you as it can. There are no adverts, no tracking and no analytics. This policy explains what personal information we hold, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

In short

  • Without an account, what you watch stays on your device.
  • With an account, we keep what's needed to sync your devices, and nothing more.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising, and nothing about you is decided automatically.
  • You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time.

Who we are

DizziTV is run by an independent developer, who is the controller of your personal information: the one responsible for deciding how it's used and for keeping it safe. To contact us about anything in this policy, use "Report a problem" in the app's settings while signed in, so we know it's you and can reply. You can also open an issue on GitHub, but issues are public, so don't put anything private in one.

What stays on your device

The app keeps your addons, settings, profiles, watch progress, My list, any service keys you add, and a short log of recent activity on the device itself. This is needed for the app to work, it's never sent to us unless you sign in or send a problem report, and it's removed when you uninstall the app.

If you make an account

An account is optional. You only need one to sync between devices. If you sign in with Google, we hold:

  • From Google: your Google account ID, email address, name and the link to your profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Your devices: a name for each device you sign in on, and when each was last used.
  • What you sync: your addons, settings for each kind of device, profiles, My list, and how far you've got through each film and episode.
  • Your service keys (such as TorBox, Real-Debrid or MDBList), encrypted before they're stored.

Watch party

You don't need an account to join a party. While a party runs, it holds the name and picture each person chooses to show, what each person is looking at in the app, what's playing and how far through, and the chat. It's held only in the server's memory, is never saved, and is gone once everyone has left. Anyone with the party's code can see it.

Problem reports

A report is only sent when you choose to send one from the app. It includes what you write, the app version, your kind of device, and the app's recent activity log and playback details, with links to streams removed on your device before sending. If you're signed in, it also includes your email address and device name, so we can reply.

Our server's records

Like any web service, our server keeps a short technical log of requests (which part of the service was used, the result and when), which we use to keep it running and secure. It doesn't log what you sync.

Why we can use your information

UK data protection law says we need a lawful reason to use personal information. Ours are:

  • To provide what you ask for (performing our contract with you, our Terms): your account, syncing your devices, watch parties, and replying to problem reports.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping DizziTV secure, preventing abuse, and finding and fixing faults: the server log, rate limits, and using problem reports to fix the app. We've checked that these don't override your interests, and they use as little information as possible.

We don't use consent as our reason, and you're never required to give us personal information: if you'd rather not, use DizziTV without an account and don't send reports.

Who else sees it

We never sell your information. These companies see some of it while helping to run DizziTV:

  • Cloudflare carries all traffic to and from our server, so it handles your IP address and the requests you make, on our behalf.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app's updates, so it sees the IP address of anyone who visits or downloads.

Some features send information straight from your device to other companies, under their own privacy policies: addons (your requests for catalogues and streams), debrid services, MDBList, subtitle providers and AI providers (if you add their keys or turn them on), Twitch, YouTube and Liquipedia (for esports; YouTube may store information on your device when a video plays), and GIPHY (if you search for GIFs in a party). When content is fetched peer to peer, other people's devices can see your IP address.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

Information outside the UK

Cloudflare, Google and GitHub are based in the United States and may handle your information there. These transfers are covered by the UK–US data bridge, or by the UK's standard contract clauses. Contact us if you'd like more detail.

How long we keep it

  • Account information: until you delete your account.
  • A signed-in device: until you sign it out or remove it.
  • Watch parties: only while the party runs.
  • Problem reports: until the problem it's about has been dealt with, then deleted.
  • Server logs: only as long as they're useful for looking into faults and abuse, then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct it if it's wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it while a concern is looked into;
  • take it with you in a common format (data portability).

To delete your account and everything stored with it straight away, or to see and remove signed-in devices, sign in at sync.thestoreisclosed.co.uk. For anything else, contact us. It's free, and we'll reply within one month.

Your right to object

Where we use your information for our legitimate interests (keeping DizziTV secure and fixing faults), you have the right to object at any time. Contact us, and we'll stop unless we have compelling grounds that override your interests.

Security

Connections to our server are encrypted, sign-ins are confirmed by Google, devices sign in with their own revocable tokens, and service keys are encrypted when stored. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we'll tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires.

This website

This site uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device and has no analytics. Its pages, fonts and pictures all come from the site itself, hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, logs visitors' IP addresses (see GitHub's Privacy Statement).

Children

You need to be at least 13 to make a DizziTV account. If you think a child under 13 has made one, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change matters to you, such as using your information in a new way, we'll tell you in the app or on this website before it takes effect.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113. If you live in the EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.
