2. What DizziTV is

DizziTV is a free media player for Windows, Android, Android TV, Samsung TVs and LG TVs. It works with addons that use the Stremio addon protocol, which supply catalogues, information about films and shows, subtitles and streams.

DizziTV doesn't host, store, upload or distribute any films, shows or other media. It comes with a few addons that supply catalogues, film and show information and subtitles. It doesn't come with any addon that supplies streams: what you can play depends entirely on the addons you add yourself. Live channels in DizziTV are schedules of shows; what plays on them comes from your addons.

DizziTV is free. We don't charge for it, show adverts in it or sell your information.
